To Phish or Not to Phish

Learn how to spot a phishing email, then take the quiz at the bottom!

What is phishing?

Phishing is when a hacker sends you a message pretending to be someone you trust, like your bank, your school, or a delivery company. They want you to click a bad link, open a bad attachment, or give them your password or personal information.

It is called "phishing" because the hacker sends out lots of bait and waits for someone to bite.

Types of phishing

  • Email phishing - fake emails sent to a lot of people at once
  • Spear phishing - a fake email made just for you, using your name or school
  • Smishing - phishing through text messages
  • Vishing - phishing through phone calls

Red flags to look for

  • Weird sender address - the name says "PayPal" but the email is from something like paypa1-secure.co
  • Urgency - "Your account will be closed in 24 hours!"
  • Suspicious links - hover over a link to see where it really goes before you click
  • Asking for private info - real companies will never ask for your password or PIN in an email
  • Generic greeting - "Dear Customer" instead of your name
  • Attachments you didn't expect - especially .zip or .exe files

What should you do?

  • Don't click any links or open attachments
  • Go to the real website yourself instead
  • Report the email or tell your IT department
  • If you already typed in your password, change it right away
